Questions before you buy

I don’t have a spare afternoon. Is 60–90 minutes realistic?
Yes. The prep block is designed as one focused session — chop, portion, freeze — not an all-day project. Many people do it Sunday morning or after a quieter weekend meal. Skip it and weeknights stay slow; do it once and the week opens up.
What if my freezer is already packed?
You don’t need a walk-in. Flat-packed portions and clear containers use space efficiently. The guide includes thaw rules so you rotate what you prep instead of stockpiling indefinitely.
Do I need special equipment?
No. A sharp knife, cutting board, basic pots and pans, and freezer-safe containers or bags are enough. If you already cook at home, you already have what you need.
Won’t this feel like meal-prep burnout?
This isn’t seven identical boxed lunches. It’s ingredient-level prep so weeknight cooking stays flexible — different dinners, shared backbone, less chopping when you’re tired. The goal is faster cooking, not identical leftovers.
What format do I receive?
An instant PDF download you can open on phone, tablet, or computer — and print pages if you prefer paper in the kitchen.
